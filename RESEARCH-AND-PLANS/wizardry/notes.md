Hermes Trismegistus
- a wizard like guy, master of alchemy, astrology, and theurgy (ritual practices aimed at invoking the divine)
- emerged during hellenistic period when greeks and egyptians met
    + **The Emerald Tablet**: A foundational text of alchemy, famous for its cryptic statement "As above, so below," which suggests a
        correspondence between the macrocosm and microcosm.
    + **The Picatrix**: A comprehensive guide to astrology and magic.
    + **The Corpus Hermeticum**: A collection of writings that discuss the nature of God, the cosmos, and the human soul.
- Hermeticism emphasizes the pursuit of gnosis (spiritual knowledge) and the idea that the material world is a reflection of a higher
   spiritual reality. It encourages self-discovery and the understanding of one's divine nature.

That sounds like a fantastic project! Here are some cool aspects you could consider adding to your wizard-themed game:

### 1. **Unique Magic Systems**
   - **Elemental Affinity:** Wizards can specialize in fire, water, earth, or air magic, with unique spells and
 abilities tied to their element.
   - **Ritual Magic:** Introduce complex spells that require multiple components and preparation, making spellcasting
 a strategic endeavor.
   - **Spell Combinations:** Allow wizards to combine spells for more powerful effects, encouraging experimentation.

### 2. **Character Customization**
   - **Skill Trees:** Create diverse skill trees that allow players to specialize in different types of magic or
 combat styles.
   - **Wizardly Backgrounds:** Players can choose a backstory that grants unique abilities or bonuses, such as a
 former scholar or a rogue mage.

### 3. **Magical Artifacts**
   - **Legendary Items:** Include powerful artifacts that grant spells, bonuses, or unique abilities, with quests to
 acquire them.
   - **Crafting System:** Allow players to gather materials and craft their own magical items, enhancing their spells
 or providing new abilities.

### 4. **Dynamic Environments**
   - **Interactive Landscapes:** Create environments that react to spellsflooding an area with water magic or
 igniting trees with fire spells.
   - **Hidden Secrets:** Incorporate puzzles that require creative spell use to unlock new areas or treasures.

### 5. **Companions and Familiars**
   - **Summon Creatures:** Players can summon fantastical creatures with unique abilities to assist them in combat or
 exploration.
   - **Companion Growth:** Allow companions to level up and evolve, gaining new abilities based on the player's
 choices.

### 6. **Dueling System**
   - **Competitive Magic:** Create a dueling arena where players can challenge each other, utilizing strategy and
 skill in real-time combat.
   - **Tournaments:** Host in-game tournaments with rewards for the best duelists to foster competition within the
 community.

### 7. **Story and Lore**
   - **Rich World-Building:** Develop a deep lore with ancient civilizations, magical wars, and legendary wizards
 that players can uncover through quests.
   - **Moral Choices:** Incorporate choices that affect the storyline, allowing players to align with good, neutral,
 or dark magical factions.

### 8. **Spellcasting Mechanics**
   - **Gesture-Based Casting:** Use gestures or combinations (e.g., mouse movements or button presses) to cast
 spells, making it more interactive.
   - **Mana Management:** Introduce a resource system where players must manage their mana or energy for powerful
 spells.

### 9. **Guilds and Factions**
   - **Wizard Guilds:** Create factions that players can join, each with unique quests, spells, and storylines.
   - **Faction Rivalries:** Introduce conflicts between guilds, leading to quests that require players to choose
 sides or broker peace.

### 10. **Environmental Challenges**
   - **Magical Anomalies:** Include areas where magic behaves unpredictably, adding a layer of challenge and
 excitement.
   - **Seasonal Events:** Host in-game events tied to seasons or celestial events that introduce limited-time spells
 and challenges.

By weaving these aspects into your game, you can create a rich and immersive experience for players who love the
 world of wizards and magic!
