

# Wands, Staffs, Anthames, Chalices
- can be made of different materials that give different properties
- certain focal pieces produce different types of magic
- depending the on piece location on the instrument, attack + direction changes which piece is activiated
- larger instruments are more powerful but slower
- smaller instruments are faster but less powerful
- rune stones can effect area of effect, duration, or visual effects.
- Spells could have modifiers like "charged", "ranged", "area effect", or "persistent", influenced by staff parts.

# Magic Sources
- magic is sourced by materials deposited in the spirit realm. 

# Spirit Realm
- the spirit realm is a condensed version of the real world
- the spirit realm can be used to transport materials as well as travel between areas
- you can build in the spirit realm as well
- looks like a crystal tree between worlds and galaxies
- distance isn't constant, on planets corresponding distances in the spirit realm are less different, but 
  between planets and galaxies small distances in the spirit realm are very large
- when planets are aligned the branches cross and more materials can be transported along the same path faster
- each player has there spirit zone that is kind of an inbetween between the real world and spirit world 



Your hair also turns white and grows long as you use more magic. It slowly goes back to normal.
