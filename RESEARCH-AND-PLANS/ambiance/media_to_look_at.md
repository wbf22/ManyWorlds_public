Hey! If you're seeking 1980s dark fantasy inspiration for your video game, there are several iconic works from that era you might find inspiring:

**Movies:**
- **Legend (1985)** — Directed by Ridley Scott, this film features a fantastical, dark fairy tale world filled with mystical creatures, darkness, and complex moral themes.
- **The Dark Crystal (1982)** — A Jim Henson puppet animation that offers a richly imagined, foreboding fantasy universe with dark undertones.
- **The NeverEnding Story (1984)** — While more whimsical, it explores dark themes of loss and heroism within a fantastical setting.
- **Excalibur (1981)** — A gritty retelling of Arthurian legend with a dark, mythic atmosphere.

**Literature and Novels:**
- Many 80s fantasy novels have darker tones; for example:
  - **Michael Moorcock’s Elric Saga** — The stories follow the albino sorcerer-antihero, Elric, in worlds filled with chaos and moral ambiguity.
  - **Clive Barker’s early works (like *The Damnation Game*, 1987)** — Known for their dark, horror-infused fantasy themes.
- **Tolkien’s works** continued to influence the dark side of fantasy themes, though their original publication was earlier, but 80s adaptations and interpretations kept the dark grandeur alive.

**Video Games:**
- The 80s saw the rise of dark-themed fantasy games:
  - **Blade Master (1988)** — A side-scrolling fantasy action game with dark imagery.
  - **Dark Castle (1986)** — A platformer with Gothic visuals and dark environments.
  - **Gauntlet (1985)** — An arcade classic featuring dark dungeons with monsters.

**Comics and Artwork:**
- 80s dark fantasy comics from artists like Mike Mignola (who started *Hellboy* later) or early works that showcase dark, gothic aesthetics.

**Music & Art:**
