Space ship max speed
- Earth is 150 million km from the sun
- the speed of light is 299,792 km/s
- we'll say the max speed of a ship is 30% of that or ~100,000 km/s
- time to sun from earth: 
    + 1500 s or 25 min
- time to moon from earth:
    + 4 s
- time to mars from earth:
    + 546 s or 9 min
- from relativity, your time on the ship will be shorter than the rest of space (1 - percent_speed_of_light^2)^1/2
    + 1 s becomes 0.9539 s on the ship
- stars in front may appear brighter, while stars behind will appear somewhat dimmer and stretched.
- KE = (
          1/(1-v^2/c^2)^1/2 - 1
       )
       * m * c^2
    + m=massOfObject kg, c=speedOfLight m/s, v=objectVelocity m/s
- Boeing 747 is about 447,700 kg
- KE of a spaceship 30% speed of light
- 0.04829 * 447,700 * 299,792^2 = 1,943,051,800,104,749 j or 1,943,051,800 mj
- average per capita energy is 43 million mj per year
- so that'd be the amount of energy for 45 people for an entire year
