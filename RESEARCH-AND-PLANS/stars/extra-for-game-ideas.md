
# SuperNova explosions
- stuff is ejected at 10,000 to 30,000 kilometers per second
- this would be almost all radiation and small particles
- You'd see the supernova flash first, 8 minutes and 20 seconds after it happened on earth
- it would take ~7,000 seconds or ~2 hours for the shockwave of radiation and particles to reach the earth
- It'd then vaporize the planet or strip away the atmosphere depending on the strength
- the earth has a diameter of 12,742 km so the shockwave would go through the earth in ~1 second

- in the milky way supernovas happen about every 50 to 100 years on average (0.013/year)
- that'd be 0.013/year * year/2,087.14hours = 0.000006372/hour in a galaxy
- In manyworlds we'd have about 2,305,840 galaxies
- so we'd have ~14.7 supernovas per hour
- or one every 4 minutes
