# Publicity Summary

## Most Important Things to Remember

### The One Sentence That Everything Hinges On (The Wedge)
> "One seamless block world, from a blade of grass to another galaxy — generated down to the furniture."

Every asset, every devlog, every screenshot serves this line. Comp comparisons (Minecraft x No Man's Sky) only appear *after* it, never as the lead.

### The Identity Problem
- Lock the name ("ManyWorlds" is weak — test collisions, add a distinctive subtitle)

### Open Source Is Your Secret Weapon
- MIT with trademark carve-out = trust story + contributor funnel
- Pin the wishlist link at the top of the README
- GitHub is quietly your second storefront (sells trust, not copies)
- Godot's community actively amplifies flagship GDExtension projects

### Never
- Use AI-generated art on the Steam page (disclosure rules + audience hostility)
- Lead with "Survival" or "Multiplayer" tags until those systems exist
- Launch a Friday
- Argue with negative reviews (fix and reply once, kindly)
- Split launch across storefronts
